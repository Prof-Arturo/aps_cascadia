# 📣 APS Voice & Style Guide

**Study:** The American Periphenomenal Survey

**Unit:** 📣 Communications  |  **Scope:** National

**Subject:** Voice, tone, terminology, and translation between internal and public-facing language

**Status:** ACTIVE REFERENCE

**Register:** Calm, precise, non-sensational. Scholarly below the waterline; plain-human above it. Never clinical to the point of coldness. Never folksy. Never sensational.

---

## 1. THE MISSION

The APS collects anomalous experience stories and, eventually, tells them. Voice is how we prove — every time someone new encounters us — that we will take their account seriously *without* sanctifying it or ridiculing it.

We do not use the language of paranormal investigation. We do not use the language of reductive debunking either. We use the language of **experience-centered folklore studies applied with contemporary tools** — steady, respectful, alert to how the story is told as much as what it says.

**The Golden Rule:** *If it sounds like a campfire story or a debunker's smirk, rewrite it until it sounds like someone taking notes with genuine attention.*

**The Iceberg Rule:** Theoretical vocabulary stays below the waterline. When these concepts appear in witness-facing materials, they are translated into plain human language.

Above the waterline it is translated. Translation is not simplification; it preserves the phenomenon while shedding the vocabulary.

## 3. TERMINOLOGY SWAP — Preferred vs. Discouraged

**In public-facing materials:**

| ❌ Do Not Say | ✅ Do Say | Why? |
|---|---|---|
| *Paranormal* | *Anomalous* / *high-strangeness* | Paranormal implies occult causation; we make no such claim |
| *Supernatural* | *Anomalous* / *unexplained* | Supernatural claims transcendence of nature; we don't |
| *Ghost hunter*, *investigator* | *Researcher*, *cartographer of the unknown* | We do not hunt; we document |
| *Believer* / *skeptic* | *Witness*, *reader*, *researcher* | Both framings are ontological commitments we avoid |
| *Subject*, *respondent*, *experiencer* | *Witness* | The person is a witness to their own experience |
| *Case* (in outreach) | *Story*, *account*, *report* | "Case" reads clinical; the person shared a story |
| *Data extraction* | *Interview*, *collection*, *conversation* | We are not extracting; we are receiving |
| *Debunk* / *disprove* | (nothing — do not use) | We do not debunk. See §5. |
| *Prove* / *verify* the phenomenon | (nothing — do not use) | We do not prove. See §5. |
| *Algorithmic synchronicity* | "Moments when digital feeds seemed to respond to unspoken thoughts" | Theory term; translate for public use |
| *The Oz Factor* | "Unnatural silence — ambient sound dropped out or felt suspended" | Theory term; translate for public use |
| *Ontological shock* | "The unsettled feeling after an experience that doesn't fit" | Theory term; translate for public use |
| *Type II / Type VI / etc.* | Describe the phenomenon plainly | Internal classification; not for witnesses |

**In internal materials, use the technical vocabulary freely.** It exists to enable precise coding, analysis, and cross-document reference. Do not translate below the waterline; that just introduces ambiguity where precision is available.

**On the witness's own words:** *Whatever the witness calls it, that is what it is called in the record.* If the witness says *skinwalker*, the record says skinwalker. If the witness says *the Being*, the record says the Being. If the witness says *a glitch in the Matrix*, the record says a glitch in the Matrix. The analyst's classification (primary_type, secondary_types, narrative_frame) is added metadata; it does not overwrite the witness's language. **This is a foundational principle, not a stylistic preference.**

## 4. THE NAMING CHOICE — Why *Periphenomena*

The project's name carries a specific philosophical commitment. In one line: **the "peri-" prefix ("around, at the edge, surrounding") positions these experiences at the periphery of ordinary experience — adjacent to it, not transcendent of it.** The Mission Statement §2.0 develops the full distinction. For voice purposes:

| Term | Prefix Meaning | What It Claims | APS Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| *Paranormal* | "para-": beyond, alongside | Experiences lie *outside* the normal; occult connotations | Rejected — makes an ontological claim we don't |
| *Supernatural* | "super-": above, over | Transcends nature entirely | Rejected — violates methodological agnosticism |
| *Epiphenomenal* | "epi-": upon, over | Causally downstream, real but inert | Used technically, ≠ synonym for periphenomenal |
| *Anomalous* | "an-" + "-homalos": not even | Deviation from expected | Accepted for public use; correct but flat |
| ***Periphenomenal*** | "peri-": around, at the edge | Adjacent to and continuous with ordinary experience | **Preferred term** |

In practice: *periphenomena* is the correct internal term for the class of experience under study. In public materials, *anomalous experiences*, *high-strangeness experiences*, or *strange experiences* are the working phrases. The word *periphenomena* itself appears in the project name and in the README gloss, but not in intake language, flyers, or the intake portal.

## 5. METHODOLOGICAL AGNOSTICISM IN LANGUAGE

Three speech acts must not be performed by anyone speaking on behalf of the project — in intake, in analyst notes, in social media, in interviews, in casual conversation.

**❌ Do not VALIDATE.**

> *"Wow, that sounds exactly like a Skinwalker."*
> *"You definitely had a genuine anomalous experience."*
> *"That's a classic Type III encounter."*

Enthusiasm for a witness's interpretation is not neutral. It steers subsequent recall and reporting. It also positions the researcher as an ontological authority we are not.

**❌ Do not DEBUNK.**

> *"That was probably just sleep paralysis."*
> *"There's a natural explanation for that."*
> *"You were likely misidentifying a bear."*

Explaining a witness's experience away foreignizes them from their own account, forecloses discovery, and violates the project's foundational principle.

**❌ Do not TRANSLATE.**

> *"So what you're really describing is a hypnagogic hallucination."*
> *"In our terminology, that would be a Type VI."*
> *"That's what folklore scholars call an ostensive act."*

Reframing a witness's account into another vocabulary — even the project's own vocabulary — *during collection* is a form of correction. Coding happens **after** collection, in a separate step, by a different eye. The original framing is preserved verbatim.

**✅ Do use — instead:**

> *"You reported..."* / *"The account describes..."*
> *"You framed the experience as..."*
> *"What did you notice next?"*
> *"What did you think it was, in the moment?"*
> *"Is there anything else about the experience that felt important to you?"*

Language that reflects the account back to the witness, invites more, and adds nothing.

## 6. CORE CONCEPTS — Below the Waterline

*These terms have precise internal meanings. Use them accurately in internal documents. In public materials, translate them per §3.*

**• Periphenomena**
- **Definition:** Experiences at the edge of the phenomenal — real, structured, and reportable, occurring adjacent to and continuous with ordinary experience without being fully accommodated by conventional scientific, psychological, or cultural explanatory frameworks.
- **Correct Usage:** *"The nine-type taxonomy classifies the range of periphenomena the archive collects."*
- **Not used publicly** except in the project name and the README's brief gloss.

**• The Iceberg Method**
- **Definition:** The APS's two-layered document architecture, in which theoretical and technical vocabulary is kept out of witness-facing materials without being abandoned as internal reference.
- **Correct Usage:** *"Per the Iceberg Method, 'Algorithmic Synchronicity' does not appear on the intake form and is translated to plain language wherever it surfaces publicly."*

**• Methodological Agnosticism**
- **Definition:** The foundational principle that the APS does not debunk or prove; it observes, collects, and archives. Premature ontological commitment in either direction corrupts the data.
- **Correct Usage:** *"The analyst_notes field is written under methodological agnosticism: concerns about a case are recorded as specific observations, not verdicts."*

**• Universal Type Collection**
- **Definition:** The principle that all nine types in the taxonomy are collected by all regional units. Regional structure is administrative and cultural-context, not phenomenological. No regional unit *owns* a type.
- **Correct Usage:** *"A Type II report from Appalachia is as archivally significant as a Type II report from Cascadia — Universal Type Collection applies."*

**• Hybrid Meaning Ecology**
- **Definition:** The theoretical framework describing how analog biological pattern-recognition and digital symbolic amplification operate simultaneously on the same nervous system, producing anomalous experience at unprecedented frequency without the ritual containment historically available.
- **Correct Usage:** *"The rise in digital-substrate cases is consistent with the hybrid meaning ecology framework developed in Mission Statement §4.3."*

**• The Oz Factor**
- **Definition:** A cross-type marker denoting unnatural environmental silence, suspension of ambient sound, or felt alteration of the sensory field. Flagged independently of primary or secondary type.
- **Correct Usage:** *"Case carries Type II primary with Oz Factor flag active; ambient silence preceded the biological encounter by an estimated 90 seconds."*
- **Public translation:** "Unnatural silence — ambient sound dropped out or felt suspended."

**• Narrative Frame**
- **Definition:** Analyst-coded metadata capturing how the witness characterizes the experience: warning, gift, test, intrusion, calling, encounter, visitation, or other. Recorded as data; not shown to witnesses during collection.
- **Correct Usage:** *"Case coded with narrative_frame = warning; the witness repeatedly returned to the phrase 'like it was telling me not to come back.'"*

**• Substrate**
- **Definition:** The material medium through which the experience registered: analog, digital, or hybrid. Recorded independently of type.
- **Correct Usage:** *"Type I case with substrate = DIGITAL; witness reports feed behavior anticipating unspoken queries across a two-week period."*

**• Ontological Shock**
- **Definition:** The acute unsettling of a person's model of reality following an experience that does not fit the frameworks they had available. Not diagnosed; described.
- **Correct Usage:** *"Debriefing addresses ontological shock via the 'Cool Down' phase; the witness is grounded before exit."*
- **Public translation:** "The unsettled feeling after an experience that doesn't fit."

**• The Grimm Protocol** (Linguistic Fidelity)
- **Definition:** The project's commitment to preserving the exact vernacular of a witness's account — stammering, confusion, code-switching, and all. The form of the telling is data.
- **Correct Usage:** *"Per the Grimm Protocol, the witness's original phrasing ('the woods went dead') is retained in the narrative field; no smoothing."*

## 7. VOICE BY DOCUMENT TYPE

Different documents carry different voice registers. Each register is load-bearing.

**Reference documents** (Mission Statement, MDP, Operations Manual). Scholarly but not bureaucratic. Willing to use a phrase like *"the semantic surface area of reality is exploding"* when it does real conceptual work. Not padded with hedges. Not written in the passive voice as a defensive posture.

**Field Manual and internal tradecraft.** Practical, direct, slightly noir. *"Treat the phone as a black mirror."* *"Park nose-out — always be ready to leave."* *"You are an observer, not a friend."* Coaching language earns its place because researchers in the field need language that stays with them.

**Debriefing Protocol and analyst-facing training.** Precise, empathetic without sentimentality, scripted where scripting protects the witness. Calibrated to a witness in a vulnerable state.

**Public-facing materials.** Calm, human, non-sensational. Not clinical to the point of coldness; not folksy. The reader should feel the project is competent without feeling it is a bureaucracy, and should feel their experience will be taken seriously without feeling they are being courted or flattered.

**Social media.** Follows public-facing rules with the added constraint that platform norms pull toward sensationalism, humor, and debate. Resist all three. The APS is not a content brand and does not compete for engagement.

## 8. TONE CHECK — Bad vs. Good

**On social media — sharing a witness account:**

❌ *"OMG check this out — witness saw a SHADOW FIGURE in Volunteer Park!!! What was it??? Ghost? Alien? Something else?? Comment below 👇👻"*

*Wrong on every axis: sensational, engagement-baiting, ontologically leading, treats the witness as content.*

✅
> **`// LOG: CAS-821`**
>
> **Location:** Volunteer Park, Seattle.
> **Type:** VI (Entity / Threshold)
> **Report summary:** Witness observed a figure near the reservoir at dusk. No physical trace. Reports similar to this location date to 1920.
>
> Full account available in the public registry.
>
> 🌲 `#APSCascadia` `#Registry`

*Calm log-header format, factual summary, no ontological commitment, points to the record rather than performing outrage or wonder.*

---

**In analyst_notes on a Type II case:**

❌ *"Witness is probably just remembering a bear."*

*Debunking. Foreignizes the witness from their account. Violates methodological agnosticism.*

❌ *"Witness had a genuine Sasquatch encounter."*

*Validating. Makes an ontological claim the project doesn't make.*

✅ *"Witness reports bipedal figure at approximately 40m, low light, no photographic evidence. Account internally consistent; no prior reporting history in this vicinity. Ambient conditions per Field Manual §2.B favorable to base-rate misidentification; noted without prejudice to the account."*

*Observational, precise about what is and isn't in the record, agnostic on ontology.*

---

**In a public description of the project:**

❌ *"The APS investigates paranormal activity in the Pacific Northwest."*

*Wrong register, wrong noun, wrong verb.*

❌ *"The APS studies periphenomena using experience-centered folklore methodology and a nine-type taxonomy."*

*Correct but above the reader's head. Theory bleeding above the waterline.*

✅ *"The APS collects and archives the anomalous experience stories of the people who live in the Cascadia bioregion — carefully, respectfully, and without deciding in advance what the stories mean."*

*Public register, storytelling recentering, methodological agnosticism implied without naming.*

---

**Talking to a witness about a Type I experience:**

❌ *"Did you experience an algorithmic synchronicity or a liminal-substrate anomaly?"*

*Theory-laden, jargon, above the waterline where it does not belong.*

❌ *"Did anything weird happen?"*

*Too casual; doesn't guide the witness toward what to report.*

✅ *"Did the location, or the time itself, feel wrong — repeating, missing, or somewhere it shouldn't be?"*

*Plain language, guides without leading, describes the phenomenon at the level of ordinary experience.*

## 10. PHRASES MARKED FOR PRESERVATION

The reference documents contain phrases that carry conceptual weight and should not be simplified during revision, editing, or excerpting. These do real work; flattening them costs meaning.

- *"the semantic surface area of reality is exploding"*
- *"personified compression algorithms"*
- *"psyche-world resonance, mechanized and operating without ritual containment"*
- *"the form of the telling is data"*
- *"cartographers of the unknown"*
- *"the mythic infrastructure of collective meaning"*
- *"the witness's own framework is preserved as primary data"*
- *"a place where strange stories are taken seriously enough to be preserved, structured, and eventually told"*

When quoted or excerpted in derivative materials (grant applications, press summaries, academic engagement), these phrases appear **intact or not at all**. Paraphrase risks slippage into the paranormal-investigation register.

## 11. SOCIAL MEDIA — Posting Templates

**Template A — Log-header post (Reddit, Bluesky, etc.):**

```
// LOG: CAS-XXX

Location: [general area — no PII]
Type: [I–IX with plain-language descriptor]
Report summary: [2–3 sentences, witness-centered, no ontological commitment]

Full account: [link to public registry entry]

🌲 #APSCascadia #Registry #[TypeTag]
```

**Template B — Terminology explainer (public-education post):**

```
[TERM in plain language, not the internal name]

Plain-language description in 2–3 sentences.
Concrete example the reader can recognize from their own life.
Why it matters — what the phenomenon tells us about how experience works.

If you have experienced this, we would like to hear from you.
→ aps-project.org

🌲 #APSCascadia
```

**Template C — Regional note (context-setting post):**

```
On [feature of the Cascadia bioregion].

[3–5 sentences of ecological, historical, or cultural context.
No ontological claims. No performance of mystery.
The reader should learn something factual and leave with
a sense of the place.]

🌲 #APSCascadia
```

**Never** open a post with all-caps sensationalism, question-bait ("Was it a ghost or an alien?"), or an emoji that performs fear or excitement (😱, 👻, 🚨). See §12 for approved emoji.

## 12. VISUAL & AUDIO STYLE

**Colors (Cascadia / Region 10):**

- **Primary:** Forest green `#29431F` (Douglas fir depth)
- **Secondary:** Steel blue (Salish Sea, Pacific fog)
- **Text on light:** Deep charcoal, not pure black
- **Text on dark:** Cream, not pure white
- **Accent:** Sparingly used; project identity should not read as "designed"

Each other region has its own primary/secondary palette per the regional visual identity system. When materials are national in scope, use the parent-mark neutral palette; regional materials use their regional palette.

**Typography:**

- **Display / logo type:** NationalPark VF (bundled in `/media/`)
- **Body type (documents):** A neutral serif or sans-serif with good readability — Georgia, Charter, Source Serif, or system defaults are all fine
- **Monospace (log headers, code, field names):** Any classic monospace — Courier, IBM Plex Mono, JetBrains Mono

**Imagery:**

- Landscape and place photography that respects the region (Douglas fir, orca, fog, coastline, mountain, forest edge)
- Documentary-register photographs of research work — clipboards, field notebooks, the archive's paper trail
- Old maps, when relevant to a specific report or region
- **Not:** stock "spooky" imagery, red-tinted flashlight photos, silhouettes on ridgelines under moons, occult symbols, staged ambiguity

**Approved operational icons and emoji:**

| Icon | Meaning | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| ✒️ | Administration | Document headers, project governance |
| 📋 | Field Research | Field-facing documents, researcher role |
| 💾 | Data Archive | Data-layer documents, schema references |
| 📣 | Communications | This function's documents |
| 🌲 | Cascadia (Region 10) | Regional identification |

**Discouraged:** performance-of-fear emoji (😱 👻 💀 🎃), engagement-bait emoji (👀 🔥 💯), any emoji that reads as sarcastic or ironic in context.

**Audio (podcasts, video voiceover, future storytelling outputs):**

- Voice: calm, human, not affected. Neither breathy-mysterious nor news-anchor-performative. Speaks *to* the listener, not *at* them.
- Music: minimal, non-manipulative. Ambient, natural sound, or nothing. Silence is preferable to underscoring.
- **Never:** dramatic musical stings, whisper effects, distorted vocals, "true crime" register.

## 13. REVIEWING EXTERNAL CONTENT

When someone outside the project writes about the APS — journalist, academic collaborator, regional partner, guest contributor — three checks apply. Requests for correction should be polite, specific, and framed as voice consistency rather than editorial control.

- **Framing check.** Does the piece use *paranormal*, *supernatural*, or *believer/skeptic* framing about the project? If yes, request a correction. These framings misrepresent the project's methodological position.

- **Tagline check.** Does the piece paraphrase the project's tagline material? If yes, request that taglines be quoted verbatim or omitted. Paraphrased versions consistently drift toward the paranormal-investigation register.

- **Witness dignity check.** Does the piece describe witnesses in a way we would not describe them ourselves — as characters, subjects, claimants, or sources of amusement? If yes, request a correction. Witnesses in APS materials are people who reported something.
